Biography

About me

Dr Theivendiram Pranavan is a Lecturer in the Department of Electrical and Computer Engineering at the National University of Singapore. His academic work combines university teaching, curriculum development, student mentorship and research in machine learning and computer vision.

He completed his PhD at the NUS School of Computing, where his doctoral research investigated learning principles inspired by the human brain, including continual learning, recency, similarity-based learning and predictive coding. Earlier, he earned a bachelor’s degree in Computer Science and Engineering from the University of Moratuwa, Sri Lanka.

His teaching experience covers discrete structures, data structures and algorithms, machine learning, programming methodology and software engineering. He is especially interested in making difficult abstractions concrete through carefully sequenced examples, practical exercises and transparent assessment design.
